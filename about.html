Frequent Asked Questions

When will you be graduating from SDSU?

What do you have planned for after graduation?

Post graduation, I will be finally sleeping in haha. I hope to apply for Physician Assitant (PA) school. What was your favorite class you took at SDSU?

I have mixed feelings on what is my favorite class in SDSU. I used to love being in the ceramics classes but besides the problematic environment (both socially and physically) it was more stress than what it was worth and would not recommend that studio to anyone. The ceramics department was a huge part of my studio art major but putting that aside, I would have to say my favorite class is this one I am taking right now! and another class that is an honorable mention is my bio for sex class, we cracked endless jokes in there haha

What work are you most proud producing at SDSU?

The work I am most proud producing at SDSU is a tie between this cockroach costume I made for the biodiversity club Bug prom costume contest or my series of work in my last ceramics class. For the cockroach costume, I've always wanted to make a cockroach costume and it was extremely fun to make. To my surprise, I won first place in the costume contest and I was extremely awkward crawling up there not expecting that outcome but I would love to make an improved version some day soon. As for my ceramic work, it was a series on attachment styles. I feel i really pushed myself with the forms i created that semester and I was pleased with all of them. Bonus points, I was able to indirectly diss my ex right infront of them when talking about the bad attachment styles as they were taking the class as well.
